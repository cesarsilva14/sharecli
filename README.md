## 🔗 Creating Your First Short Link

To share something, you tell sharecli where the item is. For example, if you have a Markdown document on your Desktop, you point sharecli at that file. sharecli reads it, publishes it, and returns a short link.

The link you receive will look like a short web address on your chosen domain. Anyone who opens it sees your page or file, and nothing else. They do not need an account, and they do not need sharecli installed.

A simple habit that helps: give your files clear names before sharing them. A document called "team-update-march" produces a tidier link than one called "untitled-3-final-v2".

## 🌐 Using Your Own Domain

One of the best parts of sharecli is that links can live on a domain you control. If you have a personal or business web address, sharecli can publish links under it so every share stays on brand.

Setting this up means telling sharecli which domain to use and confirming that the domain is connected to Cloudflare. sharecli will prompt you through the details during setup. If you get stuck, the sharecli community and Cloudflare's own help pages both cover the connection process well.

If you do not own a domain, you can skip this entirely and share links using the default address. Nothing else in sharecli changes.

## ❓ Troubleshooting

If something does not work, work through these checks calmly:

- The download did not start. Return to the releases page and try the link again, or try a different browser.
- The file will not open. Check whether the download completed fully, then download a fresh copy.
- Setup asks for a sign-in you do not have. Create the free Cloudflare account first, then restart setup.
- Your link does not load. Wait a minute and refresh, since newly published links sometimes take a moment to appear.
- Your own domain does not work. Confirm the domain is connected to Cloudflare, then repeat the domain step in sharecli.

## 🛡️ Privacy and Safety

Share only what you intend to be public. A short link is easy to pass along, so treat every link you create as something that could be forwarded.

Before sharing, check that your document does not contain passwords, personal details, or private notes. If you share a file by mistake, remove it as soon as you notice.
